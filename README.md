# 1 Min Workout

Simple group video chat with a synced 90-second workout countdown.

1. Open the site and click **Create room link** — a unique `?room=` link is generated and copied to your clipboard.
2. Share the link. Everyone who joins gets an equal share of the screen.
3. Each person clicks **I'm ready**. Once everyone is ready, a 90 → 0 countdown starts and the number is spoken every 5 seconds.

Static site (GitHub Pages). Peer-to-peer WebRTC video with serverless signaling via [Trystero](https://github.com/dmotz/trystero) (Nostr relays).
